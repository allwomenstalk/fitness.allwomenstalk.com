# 9 Inspiring Yoga Instagram Accounts to Follow for Daily Motivation

Do you consider yourself a yogi? Last year I participated in a monthly yoga challenge and loved the process. I’ve tried several types of yoga over the years from Kundalini to Vinyasa. I even took a hot sweaty Bikram yoga class, but that wasn’t really my style. During my monthly yoga challenge I found several yoga instagram accounts that inspired me to push myself harder and stay consistent. Whether you are searching for an instagram yoga girl for pose inspiration, creative yoga posts for instagram, or uplifting yoga quotes for instagram to boost your morning flow, social media is full of incredible creators. Here are 9 of my favorite inspirational yoga accounts to follow today.

Key benefits of following inspiring yogis on social media:

- Daily pose tutorials and alignment tips

- Mindful motivation and positive mindsets

- Community challenges to build consistency

## 1. @GypsetGoddess

Caitlin Turner describes herself as "just a girl with big dreams who woke up one day and decided to go for it. Live a life that you love, everyday." Her flexibility is impressive, but what I found even more impressive is her genuine joie de vivre and authentic energy. If you are looking for an instagram yoga girl who brings travel, art, and movement together seamlessly, her page offers endless creative inspiration. She frequently hosts community movement challenges that encourage followers to set mindful daily intentions, much like the practice shared by [impressive yoga instructors](#4) across the platform.

## 2. @EvolveFitWear

![Evolve Fit Wear activewear inspiration](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/l6/rm/dcbnl_human-action_sports_physical-fitness_thigh_arm_600x450.jpg)

[Evolve FIT Wear](https://www.evolvefitwear.com) is recognized as a standout brand in yoga and activewear outfit inspiration. This account allows you to tag your photos with #evolvefitwear for a chance to be featured on their main page. I met the woman behind this company years ago when she hired me to model for a yoga clothing line in Santa Monica. She’s lovely, and her brand continues to highlight real practitioners showing off comfortable activewear and practical pose breakdowns.

## 3. @HolyYogaMinistries

![Holy Yoga Ministries worship and yoga practice](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/of/cy/hgfx6_clothing_person_people_human-positions_sitting_500x500.jpg)

Holy Yoga Ministries is described as "100% Jesus and 100% yoga. Holy Yoga is worship created to deepen our connection to Christ." I found this account inspiring because it’s unusual to see Christian faith and physical yoga practice combined so intentionally. The physical practice of yoga is taken seriously while honoring spiritual reflection, offering unique yoga caption ideas and quiet reflection prompts for faith-focused yogis.

## 4. @KinoYoga

![Kino MacGregor KinoYoga instructor pose](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/gw/tz/kjybi_hair_person_woman_girl_photography_600x400.jpg)

[Kino MacGregor](https://kinoyoga.com) is certainly one of the most impressive yoga instructors on Instagram. She describes herself as a yoga teacher, author, YouTuber, and inspirational speaker. She encourages her followers to practice daily yoga, offering technical form tips alongside encouraging yoga instagram quotes. You have to see her posture tutorials to appreciate her incredible strength and flexibility.

## 5. @YogaInspiration

![Yoga Inspiration daily poses and quotes](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/kn/ei/p9al4_human-action_physical-fitness_arm_leg_sports_500x498.jpg)

Yoga Inspiration is exactly what it sounds like. This account posts daily inspirational yoga quotes and poses for the everyday yogi looking to stay grounded. It is a fantastic resource if you need a quick yoga caption for instagram for girl creators or simply want visual reminders to pause and breathe throughout a busy workday, much like the ocean flows featured by [inspiring yogis sharing ocean flows](#8).

## 6. @SpiritualGangster

![Spiritual Gangster yoga apparel](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/z5/gv/qsl0w_leisure_photo-shoot_swimming-pool_gste_spiritual_500x500.jpg)

[Spiritual Gangster](https://www.spiritualgangster.com) describes their account as "Yoga inspired clothing for high vibration living." They donate a meal to a person in need for every item sold. The first time I saw a Spiritual Gangster item of clothing, I was in Hawaii and the girl in front of me in a coffee shop had it wrapped around her waist. I had to snap a quick photo because I needed to get my hands on one. This is a stylish activewear line that brings positive energy to any workout routine, regardless of your fitness style.

## 7. @Yoga_Digest

![Yoga Digest quotes and education](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/cc/pa/ro56w_sports_physical-fitness_endurance-sports_weight-training_physical-exercise_500x501.jpg)

Yoga Digest is packed with thoughtful guidance for wellness enthusiasts. Their bio states, "Enlightenment, Education & Positive Growth." This account shares daily inspirational quotes, yoga quotes, and general wisdom for practitioners of all experience levels. It is a great place to find yoga status for instagram ideas while learning deeper health habits, providing daily inspiration alongside [faith-focused yoga practices](#3).

## 8. @Yoga_Girl

![Rachel Brathen Yoga Girl SUP yoga pose](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/td/et/au8qg_human-action_vacation_sea_physical-fitness_sports_500x501.jpg)

[Rachel Brathen](https://www.yogagirl.com) is the creator behind Yoga Girl, and her central message is simple: "I want you to love you." I first noticed her because she seemed to be the only instagram yoga girl doing complex poses on a surfboard in the open ocean. Finding balance on solid ground is challenging enough, so seeing her master SUP yoga on water is truly captivating. She shares candid reflections and daily poses that inspire millions of followers worldwide.

## 9. @Hyogini

![Heidi Hyogini mother yogi pose](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/q7/b1/w6lc4_human-action_sports_physical-fitness_arm_muscle_500x501.jpg)

I connected with Heidi during my previous yoga challenge because she was participating alongside the rest of our online community. I was so encouraged by this dedicated mother and avid yogi. She’s kind, humble, and diligent—months after our formal challenge ended, she continues posting real, authentic poses that show steady long-term progress.

Can you think of any other yoga accounts you would add to this list? I’d love to know about them in the comments below.
