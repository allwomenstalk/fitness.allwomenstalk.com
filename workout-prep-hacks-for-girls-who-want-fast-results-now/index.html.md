# Workout Prep Hacks for Women Who Want Fast Results

If you prep for your workouts, you will be on the fast track to super success. Planning is everything, and in fitness, this is most definitely the case. With the right workouts planned, food prepped, and even having your gear all set out, you help yourself stay on the right path to achieving fast results. As a certified trainer, I have helped thousands of women do just this to achieve balance and better health. Let me help you set up these essential preps pronto!

## 1. Set up Your Weekly Workout Schedule

Set up your weekly workout schedule in your phone with recurring alarms to remind you of your training sessions. Block out set times each day to get your workout in. No ifs, ands, or buts—this is your dedicated time to burn calories and release the stress of the day. Remember that scheduling workouts earlier in the day often works best because there are fewer distractions to get in your way. Following recommendations from health authorities like the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov) for regular exercise, consistent scheduling ensures you stay on track. Pairing your training schedule with [pre-prepped nutritious meals](#3) gives you the momentum you need for lasting results.

- Set daily phone reminders for specific workout times
- Block out dedicated 30 to 45 minute fitness slots
- Treat your workout appointments like non-negotiable meetings

## 2. Cut up Veggies

Cut up veggies and store them in the refrigerator in tightly closed glass or plastic containers. Being prepared when hunger strikes provides your body with the clean fuel it needs while helping curb late-night cravings. Sliced bell peppers, broccoli florets, cucumbers, and celery sticks make healthy food choices effortless throughout a busy week.

- Bell pepper strips and cucumber slices
- Celery sticks and carrot coins
- Broccoli florets and sugar snap peas

## 3. Grill Chicken and Steam Brown Rice for Week

Grill several pieces of chicken and store them for the upcoming week. Having batch-cooked protein ready enables you to quickly assemble whole wheat wraps, high-protein chicken salads, or balanced dinner plates paired with steamed brown rice and veggies. Proper meal preparation keeps you eating healthy without having to worry about what to make for dinner after a long day. For expert nutritional guidance on meal planning, resources from the [Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics](https://www.eatright.org) offer fantastic tips.

| Prep Item | Portion Size | Storage Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grilled Chicken | 4-6 oz | Airtight container (up to 4 days) |
| Steamed Brown Rice | 1/2 cup | Portion individually for quick grab |
| Sliced Veggies | 1 cup | Add a paper towel to absorb moisture |

## 4. Make Your Protein Shake and Put It in a to Go Container

Make a protein shake to enjoy right after your workout and store it in a convenient to-go shaker bottle so you can take recovery fuel wherever you go. Consuming protein within 30 to 45 minutes post-workout aids in muscle recovery and rebuilds muscle fibers, making your shake a superpowered way to restore your energy. According to exercise specialists at [ACE Fitness](https://www.acefitness.org), post-exercise nutrition is key for optimal recovery. Combining your protein recovery with [fresh sliced veggies](#2) throughout the day ensures complete daily nutrition.

## 5. Go Social and Challenge Your Friends

There is nothing like putting your fitness goals out there for everyone to see to boost your motivation. Sharing your journey on social media can inspire friends and family while creating fun accountability challenges. Setting public goals is one of the best ways to stay committed because you build accountability beyond just yourself.

## 6. Lay out Your Workout Clothes the Night before

Lay out your workout clothes the night before so that when you wake up, you can grab your fitness gear and go immediately. If you exercise bright and early, having your apparel and sneakers laid out is integral to building a seamless morning routine. Practicing this simple habit consistently helps transform exercise into an automatic daily routine. Maintaining consistent workout gear prep alongside [route hydration strategies](#7) builds a reliable, stress-free fitness lifestyle.

## 7. Store Water along Your Route

An easy prep to help you stay hydrated and commit to longer workouts is storing bottled water along your running or walking route. As a marathoner, I have experienced great results with this simple technique. Keeping extra hydration handy allows you to push through longer runs safely and reach your performance goals faster. For more endurance strategies, runners can check guides on [Runner's World](https://www.runnersworld.com).

So follow these tips and get into a super fitness routine so you can achieve ultra-amazing results! Are you ready for the challenge?
