# 7 Best Workout DVDs for Women to Transform Your Home Routine

Are you looking for some **unique workout videos** to freshen up your routine? Get all the benefits of a guided fitness class and the personalized touch of a trainer simply by popping a classic exercise disc into your player. Snagging a few of the **best workout dvds** on eBay or Amazon is an affordable way to stay fit and kiss expensive monthly gym membership costs goodbye!

Do you like to customize your routine with something out of the ordinary? Me too! While everyone else is stuck in line for a treadmill at the gym or jogging around the neighborhood in the rain, you can enjoy an effective session right in your living room.

If you want to spice up your routine and discover the **best fitness dvd** options for your home library, the following recommendations offer effective, fun, and memorable ways to sweat.

## 1. Autumn Calabrese Country Heat Dance Workout

If you are a beginner to intermediate exerciser who loves high-energy country music, you will thoroughly enjoy the Autumn Calabrese Country Heat Dance Workout DVD. This program is ideal for anyone searching for the **best dance workout dvd** options that blend low-to-medium intensity cardio with simple, catchy choreography. Experienced exercisers can also use it on active recovery days when they want a fun, lighthearted routine. For official program details and streaming options, check out [Beachbody On Demand](https://www.beachbodyondemand.com/). If you prefer Polynesian rhythms over country beats, check out the [Polynesian-inspired dance routine](#2) below.

## 2. Hot Hula Fitness

The popularity of Hot Hula Fitness has spread throughout the US, Australia, New Zealand, Europe, and Asia. Inspired by founder Anna-Rita Sloss's Polynesian heritage, Hot Hula Fitness is a low-impact, total-body workout incorporating traditional and modern dance movements from the South Pacific Islands, including Hawaii, Tahiti, Bora Bora, and Samoa. For exercisers seeking the **best low impact workout dvds** to use at home, this routine offers a fantastic core-focused workout without pounding your joints. If you ever want a change of pace from dancing, take a look at the [at-home walking exercises](#6) for gentle cardio.

## 3. The Lisa Whelchel’s Everyday Workout

This is an everyday workout created for busy women seeking practical home fitness. The DVD features a variety of personal training tips that help boost your energy and fitness levels in just a few minutes a day when scheduled efficiently. The movements are safe, effective, and tailored primarily for beginners, though intermediate exercisers will still find a solid session here.

Key features to look for in at-home fitness routines like this include:

- Clear instructor guidance and adaptable pacing for all levels
- Minimal equipment requirements, such as an exercise mat and light dumbbells
- Balanced routines that build endurance while keeping workouts engaging

Using a quality mat and light hand weights adds extra resistance for upper-body tone, making this one of the **best workout dvd for women** starting their fitness journey.

## 4. The Tank Top Arms and Bikini Belly Workout

Created by fitness instructor Minna Lessig, this targeted program focuses on sculpting sleek upper-body muscles and toning your midsection. As the title suggests, the routine combines targeted strength exercises to help build confidence for summer outfits and swimwear. Minna brings an encouraging, highly focused coaching style that keeps you motivated through every repetition. If you are looking for complementary express routines, explore the [quick ten-minute exercise sessions](#5) to maximize your daily calories burned.

## 5. The Women’s Health Total Workout in Ten

Directed by celebrity trainer Amy Dixon, this total-body program helps you complete an intense workout in convenient 10-minute segments. By breaking workouts into manageable blocks, you retain complete control over your schedule while maximizing fat burning and stamina. It stands out as one of the **best fat burning workout dvd** options for busy schedules, allowing you to focus on specific body areas or stack modules together for a full-body routine. Learn more about fitness strategies on the official [Women's Health Magazine](https://www.womenshealthmag.com/) website.

## 6. Leslie Sansone Walk Away the Pounds

Leslie Sansone has been a trusted leader in home fitness for over two decades by turning simple walking into a highly effective exercise system. Having produced over 100 DVDs, her routines combine low-impact walking intervals with light upper-body movements. The step-by-step instructions are clear and accessible for exercisers of any age. For more information on Leslie Sansone's walking programs, visit the official [Walk at Home](https://walkathome.com/) resource. To compare how different home fitness styles fit into your routine, review the comparison below:

| Country Heat Dance | Beginner to Intermediate | Fun High-Energy Cardio |
| Hot Hula Fitness | All Levels | Low-Impact Core & Sculpting |
| Total Workout in Ten | All Levels | Quick Express Fat Burning |

## 7. Joyce Vedral: Vertical Abs & Fat-Zapping Workout (3 Workouts on 1 DVD)

This versatile 3-in-1 DVD offers two targeted approaches: a deliberate, controlled routine for standing abdominal work and a faster-paced fat-zapping session designed to boost metabolic rate. You can perform both routines standing upright without ever having to lie down on the floor, making it an efficient 10-minute daily option for active home fitness.
