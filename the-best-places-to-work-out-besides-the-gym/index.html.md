# 7 Best Places to Workout Outside the Gym for Every Fitness Level

Going to the gym is definitely one of my least favorite things. While staying active and getting regular exercise feels fantastic, I much prefer to do it anywhere but inside a crowded fitness center. Dealing with peak-hour crowds, loud music, and abhorrent wait times for a single machine can instantly ruin your motivation. If you feel the same way, you will be happy to know there are loads of convenient places to workout without making the experience any more stressful than it needs to be. Exploring alternative places for exercise allows you to customize your routine, stay consistent, and actually enjoy moving your body. Try one of these top-rated workout locations next time you want to get moving—you might just decide you love exercising on your own terms!

## 1. Spread out Your Equipment in the Living Room

I find it tons easier to get through a workout when I am distracted by my favorite television show. You can do lots of types of exercises in the comfort of your own living room. Try yoga, kettlebells, weights, resistance bands or body weight moves like pushups and crunches. The options are wide open so you’ll never get bored with it. Creating a dedicated workout space at home is easily one of the best place to workout at home options available because it requires zero travel time and offers complete privacy. If you want to switch up your routine without spending money, cross-referencing your routine with outdoor options like [hitting your neighborhood streets](#2) can give you the perfect balance of indoor and outdoor fitness.

Here are a few versatile equipment items you can keep in your living room:

- Yoga mat for stretching and core work
- Adjustable resistance bands for strength training
- Kettlebells or light dumbbells for resistance moves
- Stability ball for core balance exercises

## 2. Hit the Streets in Your Neighborhood

If walking, running or biking is your exercise of choice, the streets of your neighborhood are the ideal place to get in a workout. You can take the same route every time or mix things up by taking different paths each time you head out. You’ll be getting fresh air and burning calories without being stuck inside. Outdoor cardio sessions are fantastic places to exercise because they naturally boost your mood through exposure to natural sunlight. Plus, if you ever get tired of pavement pounding, you can always alternate with [running at a local high school track field](#7) for a softer running surface.

| Workout Location | Best For | Key Advantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home Living Room | Bodyweight & dumbbells | Zero commute & complete privacy |
| Neighborhood Streets | Running, walking & cycling | Fresh air & completely free |
| Community Playground | Incline moves & step-ups | Interactive & kid-friendly |

## 3. Grab Your Ball and Go to the Nearest School Basketball Court

I do this all the time with my kids. When school is out, you can use the court (though I wouldn’t recommend trying when school is in session). Challenge your best friend or your boyfriend to a game of one on one. You’ll be having so much fun winning that it won’t even feel like you’re also working out. Basketball works your muscles and burns calories so it’s a great option. For official rules, court dimensions, and game tips, check out [USA Basketball](https://www.usab.com) for inspiration before your next pick-up game.

## 4. Take Your Kids to the Playground, but Don’t Sit down

They beg you to walk to the playground all the time, which is good exercise on its own. Once you get there, resist the urge to lounge on a bench. Instead chase the little ones around. If they don’t want you to play, do pull ups on the bars, run the steps or use the end of the slide to do incline pushups. Much like doing bodyweight exercises in your [living room setup](#1), playground equipment provides endless creative opportunities for functional strength training.

## 5. Use Your Backyard for More than a Barbecue with Friends

Sure, lounging out in the yard is great when you want to enjoy a margarita or a quick game of toss with your kids. You can also use it to fit in a workout. I love this idea because I hate when people watch me exercise so you won’t have to worry about the prying eyes of your neighbors. Run laps around the lawn or use it do jump squats or jump rope. If you need public outdoor space with more room to stretch out, combining backyard routines with [neighborhood street walks](#2) creates a dynamic workout plan.

## 6. Join a Dance Studio and You’ll Always Have a Place to Exercise

Dancing is a top notch form of exercise. You burn calories and work the muscles throughout your body. Join a dance class or become a member at a dance studio and you always have a great place to fit in a workout. Try ballet, swing or ballroom dancing. It’s a fun way to stay fit and active and you’ll make loads of great friends. You can search for certified studios and local adult beginner classes on [Mindbody](https://www.mindbodyonline.com) or visit [Fred Astaire Dance Studios](https://www.fredastaire.com) to find structured dance programs in your area.

Some popular dance styles to explore for fitness include:

- High-energy Hip-Hop or Cardio Dance
- Classic Ballet Barre for core stability and posture
- Upbeat Salsa and Latin Rhythm dancing
- Standard Ballroom for graceful full-body conditioning

## 7. Take a Step Back in Time and Head to the Nearest High School Track Field

Again, this is probably not a good idea when the kids are at school, but during the early morning hours and on the weekends, it’s a prime choice. You can use the track for a run or you can climb the bleachers to work your lower body and blast mega calories. You might even be able to get away with jumping hurdles. For official health guidelines on recommended weekly cardio and strength targets, consult the [CDC Physical Activity Guidelines](https://www.cdc.gov/physicalactivity/) to ensure your outdoor training hits all the right marks.

Where do you like to exercise? My living room is my usual choice. Do you think you’ll try any of these places next time you work out?
